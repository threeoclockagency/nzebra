Launched in September 2023, the SHERLOCK project aims to address the skills gap in energy efficiency evaluation by designing an innovative educational microprogramme. Its goal is to equip professionals in financial institutions and technical companies in the building sector with green and digital skills crucial for success in the energy efficiency investment sector.

Coordinated by UNIGE, Universita Degli Studi Di Genova (Italy), the project counts 17 European partners and will last 36 months.
