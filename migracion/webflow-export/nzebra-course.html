The nZEBRA course

The nZEBRA course is designed to empower learners in developing innovative solutions for implementing nZEB and ZEB concepts in the building environment. This intensive, blended course on climate-neutral buildings, built on micro-credentials, has the potential to be accredited as either an elective or mandatory component of Master's programmes, offering specific ECTS credits.
