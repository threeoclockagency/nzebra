We are pleased to welcome participants to the final nZEBRA stakeholder hybrid meeting, marking an important milestone as the project reaches its conclusion. Together with the closing activities of the Master’s course, the event creates an inspiring and collaborative space where students, researchers, industry professionals, and public authorities come together to exchange ideas and explore how nearly zero-energy buildings (nZEB) can be developed and implemented in practice.

Over its lifetime, the nZEBRA project has focused on developing an intensive blended learning course on climate-neutral buildings, designed to respond to new regulatory requirements and the increasing complexity of nearly zero-energy building design and implementation. The final stakeholder meeting reflects the project’s core ambition: to bridge education, research, and real-world practice, and to support the transition toward a more sustainable and climate-neutral built environment.

#### Sign up for the stakeholder meeting on 19 March

Do you work in the building and construction sector, or are you simply interested in sustainable nearly zero-energy buildings? This stakeholder meeting is open to all.
