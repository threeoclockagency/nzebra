After three years of European collaboration, the **nZEBRA project** is coming to an end, leaving behind new educational resources, practical learning experiences and a blended training programme designed to equip the next generation of building professionals with the skills needed for a climate-neutral built environment.

Co-funded by the **Erasmus+ Programme**, nZEBRA brought together six European partners with a common ambition: to strengthen education and skills in nearly Zero-Energy Buildings (nZEB) and climate-neutral building design.

#### Building skills for climate-neutral buildings

At the heart of nZEBRA was the development of an **intensive blended course on climate-neutral buildings**, combining online education with problem- and challenge-based learning.

The 7.5 ECTS programme addressed key topics including climate resilience, circular economy, energy management, smart buildings, indoor climate and life-cycle assessment, giving students the opportunity to apply their knowledge to practical and multidisciplinary building challenges.

The project also developed educational resources, including a **Train-the-Trainer toolkit**, to support educators and encourage the future uptake of the nZEBRA learning approach.

#### From Hamburg to Halmstad

The course was tested through **two Summer Schools**, providing students from different European universities with an intensive international learning experience.

Following the first Summer School at the **Hamburg University of Technology in 2025**, participant feedback was used to further improve the course and its learning activities.

The **second and final Summer School**, hosted by **Halmstad University from 8–12 June 2026**, combined academic sessions with teamwork, practical activities and site visits. Students explored sustainable renovation, climate resilience, life-cycle assessment, resource efficiency and indoor climate before completing the 7.5 ECTS course.

Throughout the project, stakeholder engagement also helped connect the educational programme with the evolving needs of the building and energy sectors.

#### A legacy beyond the project

Although nZEBRA is formally coming to an end, its educational resources and lessons learned will remain available to support students, educators and professionals.

The project demonstrated how **micro-credentials, blended learning and interdisciplinary education** can contribute to developing the skills needed for Europe's transition towards a climate-neutral building stock.

The nZEBRA consortium would like to warmly thank all the **students, educators, researchers, industry representatives and stakeholders** who contributed their knowledge, feedback and enthusiasm throughout the project's journey.

A special thank you goes to all six project partners for three years of collaboration and commitment.

**The project may be ending, but the knowledge, skills and collaborations developed through nZEBRA can continue supporting the transition towards a more sustainable built environment.**
