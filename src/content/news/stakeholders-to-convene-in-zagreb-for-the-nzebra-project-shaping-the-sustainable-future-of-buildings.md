#### Press release

***Zagreb, 2 October 2025*** – HEP ESCO will host a hybrid stakeholder meeting in Zagreb on 2 October 2025 as part of the EU-funded nZEBRA project – Sustainable Future of Buildings. The event will bring together policymakers, academics, energy experts, and practitioners to explore pathways toward achieving nearly Zero Energy Buildings (nZEB) and Zero Energy Buildings (ZEB) across Europe.

The meeting will take place at HEP Headquarters (Ulica grada Vukovara 37, Zagreb),with online participation available to ensure wide accessibility.

#### Agenda Highlights

The programme will open with welcome addresses from Željko Jelačić (HEP ESCO) and representatives from the Ministry of Physical Planning, Construction andState Assets), followed by a presentation of the nZEBRA project by Sandra Magajne (HEP ESCO).

Key sessions include:

- *Best practices from Croatia in energy renovation toward nZEB standards – Margareta Zidar, Energy Institute Hrvoje Požar*

- *nZEBRA – Sustainable Future of Buildings – Bojan Milovanović,**University of Zagreb, Faculty of Civil Engineering*

- *European experiences within the nZEBRA project – Heidi Norrström, Halmstad University*

- *Key findings and lessons learnt from EU-funded projects* – *Pablo Alekxander Baigorria Kobylinski, CREARA*

A panel discussion moderated by Prof. Vlasta Zanki (University of Zagreb) will bring together experts from academia, industry, and civil society todebate the role of local stakeholders in the transition to nZEB and ZEB.

The event will conclude with a Q&A session and closing remarks by Anamari Majdandžić (DOOR) and representatives from the Ministry of Physical Planning, Construction and State Assets.

You can find the agenda [here](/documents/news/stakeholders-to-convene-in-zagreb-for-the-nzebra-project-shaping-the-sustainable-future-of-buildings/68dd1a890438e2e87321b616-nzebra-stakeholder-meeting-agenda-4.pdf).

- **Format**: Hybrid (in-person & online)
