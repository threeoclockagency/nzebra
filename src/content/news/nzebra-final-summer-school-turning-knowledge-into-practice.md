The **nZEBRA Final Summer School**, hosted by **Halmstad University**, marks a major milestone for the project and will formally conclude its activities. Building on years of collaboration, learning,and innovation, this final event brings together **students from Sweden and Germany** to take part in the **final version of the nZEBRA course**.

During the summer school, participants will engage with the full curriculum — combining **theoretical foundations and practical applications**, including exercises and hands-onexamples tailored to the building and construction sector. The programmeis designed to equip future professionals with the knowledge and skills neededto develop and implement nearly zero-energy building**s** in real-worldcontexts.

This event follows the [**first nZEBRA Summer School**](/post/reflections-from-the-nzebra-summer-school-in-hamburg), which was successfully organised last June in Hamburg by TUHH. The second and final edition will takeplace **in mid-June**, with student participation based on **specific selection criteria** to ensure an optimal learning experience.

#### Stakeholder Meeting Ahead of the Summer School

Ahead ofthe Final Summer School, a second stakeholder meeting will be held in **Halmstadon 19 March**. This meeting will focus on how sustainable and nearly zero-energy buildings can be developed and implemented in practice, bringing together experts from industry, academia, and local authorities.

Confirmed speakers include:

1. Christer Kilersjö, CEO of Eksta AB, who brings decades of experience in developing economically viable nearly zero-energy and plus-energy buildings since the 1980s

2. Christian Johansson, sustainability and indoor climate expert at EVIA, a company specialising in hempcrete and other sustainable materials

3. Mohammadreza Aghaei, Senior Professor in Renewable and Sustainable Energy

4. Johanna Runge, Architect, Municipality of Halmstad

The concluding panel discussion will be facilitated by Charlotta Winkler, ensuring an interactive exchange between speakers and participants.

The **nZEBRA Final Summer School and stakeholder meeting** reflect the project’s core ambition: to bridge education, research, and practice in support of a more sustainable built environment.
